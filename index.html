Bayou State Roleplay (BSRP)

Official Comprehensive Code of Conduct, Statutory Rules, and Operational Regulations

IMPORTANT NOTICE

By accessing Bayou State Roleplay — including our ingame server, Discord server, custom CAD/MDT systems, forums, or any affiliated platform — you automatically enter into a binding agreement to comply fully with the rules and policies outlined below. Failure to do so will result in administrative disciplinary actions up to and including immediate, permanent global removal from all current and future platforms.

Statutory Codes

1.1 Legislative Mission Statement

Welcome to Bayou State Roleplay (BSRP). BSRP stands as a premier high-fidelity, serious roleplay community. Our administration is built upon three non-negotiable pillars: uncompromising mechanical and narrative realism, proactive community-driven development, and absolute blind administrative fairness.

Unlike standard roleplay servers that operate under a static, dictatorial legal framework, BSRP utilizes an Adaptive Legislative Model. We recognize that a thriving virtual ecosystem requires evolving guidelines. Our rules are continuously audited, adjusted, and expanded based on empirical gameplay data and community feedback.

1.2 The Open Feedback Loop (#suggestions)

Every citizen of BSRP possesses an active legislative voice. Any community suggestion that maintains a net-positive approval rating above 75% over a continuous 7-day period is automatically escalated to the High Command Legislative Docket.

1.3 Adaptive Enforcement

While our statutory codes are dynamic, our enforcement is unyielding. The phrase "I didn't know the rule changed" does not constitute a valid defense. It is the absolute, personal responsibility of every community member to review the Changelog channel prior to connecting to the game servers.

To completely eliminate administrative bias, personal vendettas, and "staff power-tripping," BSRP operates under a strict, formalized judicial system derived from Anglo-American legal traditions. A staff member cannot issue a punitive verdict based on a hunch or unverified claim.

2.1 The Two-Pronged Legal Burden

To sustain a formal infraction log or ban, the presiding Staff Member must successfully prove Actus Reus (Objective, verifiable, and physical evidence that the server rule was violated) AND Mens Rea (Clear evidence of intent to disrupt the roleplay environment).
